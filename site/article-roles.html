Occupations

Occupations readers meet in this search

Job Compass · General information · Last reviewed 2 November 2026

The search mixes a few large occupations. Their pay, training, and schedules are not the same.

The short version

  • Customer service, sales support, and admin dominate entry-level remote postings.
  • Bookkeeping and medical records pay more and usually ask for some training.
  • Tutoring and some data-entry shifts are the small-hours end of the market.
  • Medians below are for the occupation in every setting, not a promise on a single ad.

High-school entry, often an assigned shift

FlexJobs counted its own listings from 1 January to 1 May 2026. Customer service, administrative work, and sales led entry-level remote hiring, and each grew more than 30% in that slice of the database. The titles posted most often were sales development representative, customer service representative, and business development representative. Employers with the most entry-level remote postings in that database included Mass General Brigham, Thermo Fisher Scientific, HCA, the State of North Carolina, T-Mobile, IQVIA, Stride, Raymond James, Centene, and Toast.

The May 2025 median for customer service representatives was $21.53 an hour. About 2.7 million people held the job. Employment is projected to decline 5% from 2025 to 2035, with openings still coming from people who leave. Home-based ads at the entry level have been clustering near $15 to $17 an hour, with a set shift and a quiet workspace. That is the bottom of the occupation’s range.

A certificate or a degree

Bookkeeping, accounting, and auditing clerks had a May 2025 median of $24.36 an hour, or $50,670 a year. Usual entry is some college and moderate training. Medical records specialists had a median of $24.59 an hour, or $51,140. Usual entry is a postsecondary certificate, and employment is projected to grow 8% from 2025 to 2035. Software developers had a median of $135,980. Usual entry is a bachelor’s degree, and a large share of those postings still mention remote or hybrid work. Interpreters and translators had a median of $28.93 an hour. Bilingual phone support is a different, often lower-paid, use of a second language.

Hours a person can keep small

Tutors numbered about 175,070 in May 2025, with a median wage of $20.84 an hour. Online tutoring ads around $18 to $22.50 an hour, for evening hours and a cap near 25 hours a week, sit close to that median. Data-entry ads around $16 an hour, including part-time shifts on a single weekday, are a second-income shape. Contract virtual-assistant work follows a client’s calendar, and the worker handles their own taxes. Delivery and rideshare are local and flexible. In the Bankrate survey they were a small share of side work.

FlexJobs, entry-level remote jobs, updated 28 May 2026. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook, May 2025 medians, and OEWS table 1 for tutors. Live ads mentioned here were reviewed in September and October 2026 and are examples of arrangements, not openings from Job Compass.
