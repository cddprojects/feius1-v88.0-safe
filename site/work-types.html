Work types

Three arrangements, kept separate

Location, delivery, and hours. A single job can include more than one.

Remote and hybrid

Remote work is done away from the employer’s usual workplace. Hybrid means some days at home and some days at a set site. A posting that requires regular office days is hybrid. Fully remote postings are a small share of ads and attract a large share of applications. Education and occupation change the odds: in the September 2026 survey week, 37.8% of people with a bachelor’s degree teleworked at least some hours, compared with 8.3% of high school graduates with no college.

Online work

Online work is completed or delivered over the internet. The person may be at home, in an office, or in another city the employer names. “Online” by itself does not say whether the hours are chosen by the worker or assigned by the employer, or whether the role is employment or contract work.

How the words differ

Flexible and part-time

Flexible usually means some choice over the schedule. In hourly jobs, that choice is often the ability to see shifts ahead of time. A home-based customer-service ad can still assign an eight-hour shift. In early 2025, 62% of employees in the American Job Quality Study lacked a predictable, stable schedule they could control.
