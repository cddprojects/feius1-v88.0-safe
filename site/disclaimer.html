Scope

Disclaimer

Limits that apply to every page. Updated 3 November 2026.

A record for reading

Weekline publishes general information about online, remote, and flexible work in the United States. The content does not consider a particular reader’s circumstances and does not recommend an arrangement.

No employment service

Weekline is not an employer, a job board, or an employment agency. It does not publish openings, receive applications, or introduce candidates. Reading the site does not create an employment, recruitment, or advisory relationship.

No professional advice

The content is not legal, tax, financial, or employment advice. Minimum wages, payroll rules, and worker classification vary by law and by facts. A decision that depends on those rules belongs with the responsible agency or a qualified professional.

No offer and no earnings promise

Nothing on the site is a job offer or a promise of earnings. Occupation medians and third-party ads illustrate the published record. They are not vacancies from Weekline, and ads expire.

Figures move

Surveys, wage tables, and proposed regulations change. Each note names a review date and a source. Readers should confirm a figure with the cited source before relying on it.

External sites

Links exist so a reader can reach the original source. Weekline does not control those sites.
