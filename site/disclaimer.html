Disclaimer

Limits that apply to every page. Last updated 2 November 2026.

General information only

Job Compass publishes general information about online, remote, and flexible work in the United States. The content is for reading. It does not consider a particular reader’s circumstances.

No employment service

Job Compass is not an employer, a job board, or an employment agency. It does not publish openings, receive applications, or introduce candidates. Reading the site does not create an employment, recruitment, or advisory relationship.

No professional advice

The content is not legal, tax, financial, or employment advice. Minimum wages, payroll rules, and worker classification vary by law and by facts. A decision that depends on those rules belongs with the responsible agency or a qualified professional.

No promise of work or income

Nothing on the site is a job offer or a promise of earnings. Examples of occupations and of expired or third-party ads illustrate arrangements. They are not vacancies offered by Job Compass.

Figures change

Surveys, wage tables, and proposed regulations move. Each guide names a review date and a source. No warranty is made that every sentence is complete or current. Readers should confirm a figure with the cited source before relying on it.

External links

Links are provided so a reader can reach the original source. Job Compass does not control those sites.
