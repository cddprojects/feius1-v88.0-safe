If a figure looks wrong

Each guide names its source and the date it was reviewed. The agency page linked from the guide is the record to check. Labor figures are updated by those agencies, and a guide can lag a release.

If a posting looks wrong

The employer named on the posting is the party that can explain the hours, the pay, and the status. A posting with no employer name matches a pattern the Federal Trade Commission tells consumers to refuse. Reports can be filed at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

No message is stored here
