Definitions

Place, delivery, and hours

The three words often share a sentence. They still name different facts.

Paid Day · Record · Reviewed 3 November 2026

In short

  • Remote describes where the work is done.
  • Online describes how the work is delivered.
  • Flexible describes the hours or the workload.
  • A job can include one of them, or all three, and still have an assigned shift.

Where

Remote work happens away from the employer’s usual site. Hybrid mixes home days and site days. Among full-time wage-and-salary workers in August 2026, 12.1% were fully remote, 27.1% hybrid, and 60.8% fully on site. A posting that requires regular office days is hybrid. Education changes the week: in the September 2026 survey, 37.8% of people with a bachelor’s degree teleworked at least some hours, compared with 8.3% of high school graduates with no college.

How

Online work is done or turned in over the internet. A hospital coder, a tutor on a video call, and a person answering calls from a spare room are all doing online work. The coder may have a certificate and a set caseload. The tutor may work evenings. The call shift may be assigned between set clock times. The word “online” does not choose among those.

When

Flexible work means some say over the schedule. Part-time work, in Bureau of Labor Statistics household data, is fewer than 35 hours. It is a count of hours, not a promise of choice. A part-time data-entry shift every Monday is regular. A contract whose hours follow a client is a different arrangement. In the American Job Quality Study, fielded 13 January to 25 February 2025 with 18,429 workers, 62% lacked a predictable, stable schedule they controlled. About 27% had unpredictable schedules, about 28% unstable ones, and 41% had little or no control. Fifty-four percent often or sometimes worked longer than planned.

Employer benefit surveys describe a related, narrower fact. SHRM’s 2026 benefits report said 57% of employers offered hybrid work, 45% offered flextime during core hours, and 10% offered a four-day week. Those are employer policies, not a count of workers at home.

Survey of Working Arrangements and Attitudes, August 2026. Bureau of Labor Statistics, table A-41, September 2026, and the full-time definition of 35 hours. American Job Quality Study, Jobs for the Future and Gallup, 2025. SHRM, 2026 employee benefits.
