Definitions

Place, delivery, and hours

The three words often appear in the same sentence. A reader can still take them one at a time.

Weekline · Record · Reviewed 3 November 2026

In short

  • Remote is the place.
  • Online is the way the work travels.
  • Flexible is the schedule.
  • A week can use one of them, or all three, and still have set hours.

Remote is the place

Remote work happens away from the usual workplace. Home is the place most people mean. A hybrid week mixes the two: some days at home, some days at the workplace. In August 2026, about 12 in 100 full-time employees worked only from home, about 27 in 100 split the week, and about 61 in 100 worked only at the workplace.

Schooling changes how common home days are. In the September 2026 survey week, about 38 in 100 people with a bachelor’s degree did at least some paid work at home, compared with about 8 in 100 people whose schooling stopped at high school.

Online is the way the work travels

Online work is done or turned in over the internet. A hospital worker updating records, a tutor on a video call, and a person answering calls from a spare room are all doing online work. Their weeks are still different. One may have a set pile of records. One may choose evenings. One may have a shift that starts and ends at a set time. The word “online” does not choose among those weeks.

Flexible is the schedule

Flexible means some say over when the work happens. Part-time is a different idea. In the federal household survey, part-time means fewer than 35 hours. A short Monday shift that happens every week is regular. It is not the same as a week whose hours follow a client, or a week a person shapes around school.

A national study of workers, taken from January to February 2025, found that 62 in 100 did not have a predictable schedule they could control. So when people say “flexible,” it helps to ask how much of the clock they actually choose. Some employers offer a limited version of that choice: in 2026, 45 in 100 employers in one benefits survey offered flextime inside core hours, and 10 in 100 offered a four-day week.

Survey of Working Arrangements and Attitudes, August 2026. Bureau of Labor Statistics, September 2026. American Job Quality Study, Jobs for the Future and Gallup, early 2025. SHRM employee benefits survey, 2026.
