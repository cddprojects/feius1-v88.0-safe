What to know before your first remote, online, or independent job.

Weekline is a reader’s companion, not a job board. These pages explain what usually happens once the work starts, so the first week has fewer surprises.

Most people trying remote, online, or independent work for the first time aren’t short on job titles. They’re short on a plain answer to a handful of ordinary questions: who actually checks on the work, whose clock decides the hours, what the pay should look like, and whether you’re an employee or on your own. The starter’s companion answers those in order, and the shorter notes below cover each piece in more depth.

The floor changes at the state line.
