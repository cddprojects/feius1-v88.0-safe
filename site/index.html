The search and the work week are different counts.

Four lines in the record

  1. 01

    Place, delivery, and hours are separate facts.

    Remote is where the work happens. Online is how it is delivered. Flexible is when the hours fall. A posting can use one word and still require a set office or a set shift. The three words.

  2. 02

    Days, people, and ads are separate counts.

    A share of paid days is not a share of workers, and a share of ads is not a share of jobs held. How the figures differ.

  3. 03

    The wage floor stops at a state line.

    The federal minimum is still $7.25. Many states require more, and a separate set of states requires a pay range on a posting. Wages and states.

  4. 04

    This site records the counts.

    Paid Day does not list openings, take applications, or tell a reader which arrangement suits them.

On site, still

August 2026, full-time employees.

Among full-time wage-and-salary workers, the same home-day survey put 60.8% fully on site, 27.1% hybrid, and 12.1% fully remote. Gallup’s higher remote share covers employees whose jobs can be done remotely, not the whole workforce.

Beside a main job

A second paycheck is a smaller count than the conversation.

In September 2026, 5.5% of employed people held two jobs. A side-hustle survey the same year, using a wider definition, found 27% of adults and a median of $200 a month. The two definitions.
