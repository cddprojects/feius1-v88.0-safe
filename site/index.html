The big picture

Four things to consider before your next career move.

📈

01

Job demand

Healthcare support is among the occupational groups projected to grow fastest through 2035, and data scientists rank among the fastest-growing individual occupations. Once a role catches your eye, check pay and openings where you live.

BLS employment projections · CareerOneStop wage tools

🎓

02

Skills and training

Start with the job you want, then compare ways to qualify. A community college program, certification, degree, or paid Registered Apprenticeship can each make sense, depending on the role.

Apprenticeship.gov career seeker resources

🧭

03

Work flexibility

In 2025, 35% of employed people did some or all of their work at home on days they worked. When comparing jobs, look at where you’re expected to work, which hours you need to be available, and how often you’ll need to go in.

BLS American Time Use Survey

💰

04

Extra income

In a 2024 Federal Reserve survey, some gig workers said they'd struggle to make ends meet without that income, and many wanted steadier pay. When comparing options, look at what you could earn after expenses and how reliably the work comes in.

Federal Reserve household survey, 2024

Extra income

What could a second job or side gig add to your budget?

A weekend shift, a freelance project, and app-based gig work each bring in extra income, and each makes different demands on your time. Start with the hours you can realistically give, then compare how often you’ll get paid, what the work costs you, and how reliably it comes in.

Practical guides

What to ask before you apply or accept an offer.

How to ask for a flexible schedule

Put together a proposal that explains the schedule you want, how you’ll cover your responsibilities, and when you and your manager can review how it’s working.

Employee or independent contractor?

Ask how you’ll be paid, who covers equipment and expenses, and which benefits, if any, come with the arrangement.

How to write a résumé for a remote role

Show how you’ve handled deadlines, shared updates, and worked with people in different locations, using specific examples of what you delivered.
