Quick answers

Before you open another tab.

These are the questions most people wonder about but rarely ask out loud. None of them are too obvious to answer. The full guides go deeper, with sources.

See every note →

Do I need to pay anything to apply?

No. Legitimate employers never charge a fee for an application, a “starter kit,” or training, and never ask you to deposit a check and send part of it back. Any of those is the clearest sign of a scam.

Will I need special equipment to work from home?

Often yes: a computer meeting basic specs and a reliable internet connection, sometimes a wired connection rather than a hotspot. A real employer ships or reimburses required equipment; it does not make you buy it upfront.

Am I an employee or a contractor?

Check which tax form you’re asked to complete. A W-4 means you’re a W-2 employee with tax withheld from each paycheck. A W-9 means you’re a 1099 contractor who handles your own taxes.

How long should an application actually take?

About half of job seekers consider 10 to 15 minutes reasonable for an online form. If it’s taking much longer, or asking you to retype a résumé you already uploaded, that’s a known frustration, not something wrong with you.
