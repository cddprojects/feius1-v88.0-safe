Why the figures disagree

Each number answers a different question.

Days

A share of the week, not a share of workers.

26.8% of paid days were at home in September 2026. The rest of those days were somewhere else.

 People

A survey week, not a preference.

21.9% of people at work that week teleworked at least some hours. Gallup’s higher remote share covers only jobs that can be done remotely.

 Ads

A thin shelf, a thick pile of applications.

About 8% of paid U.S. postings were remote and drew about 35% of applications. That is not a count of people working from home.

What the record is for

Read the fact that matches the question.

  1. 01

    Separate place, delivery, and hours.

    Remote is where the work happens. Online is how it is delivered. Flexible is when the hours fall. A home-based shift can still be assigned. The three words.

  2. 02

    Follow the wage across a state line.

    The federal minimum is still $7.25. Many states require more, and a separate set of states requires a pay range on a posting. Wages and states.

  3. 03

    Keep a second job apart from a side survey.

    In September 2026, 5.5% of employed people held two jobs. A wider side-hustle survey found 27% of adults and a median of $200 a month. The two definitions.
