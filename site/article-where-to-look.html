Looking

Where people look for this work

Job Compass · General information · Last reviewed 2 November 2026

Most looking still starts on a general job board, then moves to a company site or a professional network.

The short version

  • General job boards are the common starting place.
  • LinkedIn, search engines, and employer sites are used alongside the boards.
  • Remote ads are a small share of postings and a large share of applications.
  • A text message from an unnamed recruiter is a different channel, and a risky one.

Channels people say they use

iHire’s 2025 survey of 1,421 U.S. job seekers asked which resources they use. People could choose more than one. General job boards were named by 78.5%, professional networks such as LinkedIn by 54.9%, niche boards by 53.8%, company websites by 45.0%, and search engines by 41.6%. Referrals were 32.7%. Social media was 15.7%. Craigslist was 8.3%.

HireClix asked a different question of 1,000 adults in 2025: where a search starts. Indeed was 56%, LinkedIn 52%, Google 41%, and corporate career sites 40%. The two surveys can both be right. One asks about every channel. The other asks about the first stop.

Remote ads are the crowded shelf

Indeed’s count of postings that mention remote or hybrid work was 2.6% in January 2019, 10.4% at the February 2022 peak, and 8.3% on 31 January 2025. LinkedIn told Business Insider that in September 2025 about 8% of paid U.S. postings offered remote work and those postings drew 35% of applications. A reader who searches only the word “remote” is looking at a thin set of ads with a thick set of applicants.

Occupation changes the shelf

Insurance, legal, mathematics, and software postings mention remote or hybrid work far more often than retail, driving, or construction. A secondary compilation of Indeed’s tracker put software development near 30.8% on 31 August 2026, and driving near 1.2%. Customer service is common as an entry-level remote title even though many customer-service jobs remain on site.

iHire, State of Online Recruiting 2025. HireClix, 2025 Candidate Experience Survey. Indeed Hiring Lab, November 2024 update, and Forbes, 20 March 2025, citing Indeed. Business Insider, 19 October 2025, quoting LinkedIn. The August 2026 occupation shares come from a secondary compilation of the Indeed tracker.
