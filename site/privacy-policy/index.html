Privacy

How a visit to this site is handled. Updated 3 November 2026.

What the site is for

Weekline publishes general information about U.S. careers, pay, training, and flexible or extra-income work. It does not ask readers to create an account, upload a résumé, or submit a job application.

What a visitor may send

The contact form on the homepage and the Contact page lets you send a topic suggestion. That form asks for your name, email address, an optional phone number, and your message. Submitting it opens a message in your own email app, addressed to Weekline. Weekline uses those details only to reply to the suggestion. It does not use the form to take job applications, and it does not add visitors to a mailing list.

What the site does not collect

Pages do not ask for a Social Security number, a date of birth, bank details, or payment information.

Fonts

Pages load Inter from Google Fonts. A visit can send the usual technical request, including an IP address, to that service. A browser setting that blocks third-party fonts leaves the words of the site in the fallback typefaces.

Local features

The note filter, the term search, and the mobile menu run in the browser. They do not send what a reader types to Weekline.

Links

Notes link to public agencies and to reporting on those figures. Those sites have their own practices. A link is a citation.
