Second jobs and side work

Job Compass · General information · Last reviewed 2 November 2026

A second paycheck is common in conversation and less common in the official job count. The gap is the definition.

The short version

  • The official multiple-job rate was 5.5% in September 2026.
  • A side-hustle survey the same year found 27% of adults, using a wider definition.
  • The median in that survey was $200 a month.
  • Online selling and freelance tasks were named more often than rideshare.

The official count

In September 2026, not seasonally adjusted, 9.0 million people held more than one job. That was 5.5% of employed people. Women were at 6.4% and men at 4.7%. The usual pattern was a full-time main job plus a part-time second job: 5.2 million people. Both jobs part time accounted for 2.25 million. Two full-time jobs were uncommon, at 421,000.

Side work, defined more loosely

Bankrate’s YouGov survey of 2–4 June 2025, with 2,616 adults, defined a side hustle as extra money beyond the main source of income. Twenty-seven percent said they had one, down from 36% in 2024. The average was $885 a month and the median was $200. Among people who had one, online sales were 15% and professional services such as writing or design were 14%. Food delivery was 9%. Rideshare was 3%. Forty-three percent said none of the listed types matched what they did. Forty-one percent used at least some of the money for discretionary spending, and 35% used at least some for regular living expenses.

A wider question, any side income in the past year, produces a higher share. Intuit QuickBooks surveyed 3,000 adults in December 2025 and 47% said they had earned something. That result and the Bankrate result describe different questions. Neither replaces the Bureau of Labor Statistics count of people who hold two jobs.

What “small” looks like

Most of this activity does not replace a paycheck. In the Bankrate survey, 14% of side hustlers earned more than $1,000 a month, and 16% wanted the side work to become their main income. A posting that describes a few hours of phone work as a full salary is describing something other than this pattern.

Bureau of Labor Statistics, tables A-16 and A-39, September 2026. Bankrate press release, 9 July 2025. Intuit QuickBooks, State of Side Hustles, December 2025 survey.
