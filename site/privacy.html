Privacy

How this informational site handles a visit. Last updated 2 November 2026.

What the site is for

Job Compass publishes general information. It does not ask readers to create an account, upload a résumé, or submit an application.

Information the site does not collect

There is no application form and no mailing list on this site. Pages do not ask for a Social Security number, a date of birth, or payment details.

Fonts

Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. A visit can therefore send the usual technical request, such as an IP address, to Google’s font service. Readers who want to avoid that request can use a browser setting or extension that blocks third-party fonts. The words of the guides remain available either way.

Local features

The guide filter, the glossary search, and the mobile menu run in the browser. They do not send what a reader types in the glossary box to Job Compass.

Links

Guides link to public agencies and to reporting on those figures. Those sites have their own privacy practices. A link is a citation, not an endorsement.
