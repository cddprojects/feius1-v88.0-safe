Privacy

Privacy

How a visit to this record is handled. Updated 3 November 2026.

What the site is for

Paid Day publishes a public record. It does not ask readers to create an account, upload a résumé, or submit an application.

What the site does not collect

There is no application form and no mailing list. Pages do not ask for a Social Security number, a date of birth, or payment details.

Fonts

Pages load Newsreader and Public Sans from Google Fonts. A visit can send the usual technical request, including an IP address, to that service. A browser setting that blocks third-party fonts leaves the words of the record in the fallback typefaces.

Local features

The note filter, the term search, and the mobile menu run in the browser. They do not send what a reader types to Paid Day.

Links

Notes link to public agencies and to reporting on those figures. Those sites have their own practices. A link is a citation.
