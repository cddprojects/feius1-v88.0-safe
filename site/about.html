About

A record, not a search.

Weekline explains remote, online, and flexible work in the United States. Three different parts of the same week.

What is being recorded

The subject is three words. Remote is where the work happens. Online is how it is delivered. Flexible is when the hours fall. A week can include one of them, two, or all three.

Weekline is an independent publisher. It is not a government agency, not an employer, and not an employment agency. It does not list jobs, receive applications, or send reader information to employers.

The line

A paid day, a person in a survey week, and an advertisement are different units. Weekline keeps those units in separate columns, because they are the figures most often treated as one figure.

The limit

The record does not rank arrangements for a reader’s life, and it does not replace the Department of Labor, the Internal Revenue Service, a state labor office, or the Federal Trade Commission.

Weekline publishes general information. It has no application inbox. A question about a specific posting belongs with the employer named on that posting, or with the agency that wrote the rule.
