About

A companion, not a job board.

Weekline explains what remote, online, and independent work actually involve, in plain language, before a reader says yes to any of them.

What a reader can learn here

Remote is the place. Online is how the work travels. Flexible is the schedule. Independent is who you answer to. The pages keep those apart using everyday situations, then point to the public count behind a figure when one is used.

Weekline is an independent publisher. It is not an employer, a staffing agency, or a job board. It does not list openings and it does not take applications. Reading a page does not sign anyone up for work.

Who this is for

Someone trying remote, online, or independent work for the first time, or anyone who wants a plain answer before taking an offer that sounds too easy.

The limit

The pages do not choose a week for a reader, and they do not replace the Department of Labor, the Internal Revenue Service, a state labor office, or the Federal Trade Commission.

Weekline publishes general information for reading. It has no application form. A question about a particular job belongs with the employer who offers it.
