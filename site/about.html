About

Written for readers.

Weekline explains what remote, online, and flexible work mean in an ordinary week.

What a reader can learn here

Remote is the place. Online is the way the work travels. Flexible is the schedule. The pages use everyday weeks so those words stay separate, and they point to the public count behind a figure when a number appears.

Weekline is an independent publisher. It is not an employer and it does not list jobs. Reading a page does not sign anyone up for work.

The weeks on the front page

A daytime week at home, a week at the office, and a few evenings of tutoring are illustrations. They are not offers, and they are not stories about real named people.

The limit

The pages do not choose a week for a reader, and they do not replace the Department of Labor, the Internal Revenue Service, a state labor office, or the Federal Trade Commission.

Weekline publishes general information for reading. It has no application form. A question about a particular job belongs with the employer who offers it.
