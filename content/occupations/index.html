Which occupations the remote shelf actually holds

The shelf mixes a few large occupations. Their pay, training, and schedules are not the same, and a median is for the occupation in every setting.

Find Curate · Record · Reviewed 3 November 2026

In short

  • Customer service, sales support, and admin dominate entry-level remote postings in one large database.
  • Bookkeeping and medical records sit higher, and usually ask for some training.
  • Tutoring and some data-entry shifts are the small-hours end.
  • Software is a high median and a high remote-mention share. It is a different shelf.

High-school entry, often an assigned shift

FlexJobs counted its own listings from 1 January to 1 May 2026. Customer service, administrative work, and sales led entry-level remote hiring, and each grew more than 30% in that slice. The titles posted most often were sales development representative, customer service representative, and business development representative. Employers with the most entry-level remote postings in that database included Mass General Brigham, Thermo Fisher Scientific, HCA, the State of North Carolina, T-Mobile, IQVIA, Stride, Raymond James, Centene, and Toast.

The May 2025 median for customer service representatives was $21.53 an hour, or $44,770 a year. About 2.67 million people held the job. Employment is projected to decline 5% from 2025 to 2035, with about 289,500 openings a year from people who leave. Usual entry is a high school diploma. Home-based ads at the entry level have clustered near $15 to $17 an hour, with a set shift. That is the bottom of the occupation’s range. The all-occupations median cited on that handbook page was $24.51 an hour.

A certificate or a degree

Bookkeeping, accounting, and auditing clerks had a May 2025 median of $24.36 an hour, or $50,670 a year. Usual entry is some college. The outlook is a 6% decline. Medical records specialists had a median of $24.59 an hour, or $51,140. Usual entry is a postsecondary certificate, the outlook is 8% growth, and openings are about 14,000 a year. Software developers had a median of $135,980. Quality-assurance analysts and testers were $104,300, and the combined handbook quick fact was $134,040. Usual entry is a bachelor’s degree. The combined group’s outlook is 10% growth, on 1,905,400 jobs. Interpreters and translators had a median of $28.93 an hour, or $60,170, with a bachelor’s degree the usual entry and a 2% outlook. Bilingual phone support is a different, often lower-paid, use of a second language.

Hours that stay small

Tutors numbered 175,070 in the May 2025 wage table. Mean hourly pay was $23.10 and mean annual pay was $48,050. The median hourly wage was $20.84. The table does not print a median annual figure. Online tutoring ads around $18 to $22.50 an hour, for evening hours and a cap near 25 hours a week, sit close to that hourly median. Data-entry ads around $16 an hour, including a part-time Monday shift, are a second-income shape. A contract virtual-assistant ad at $22 an hour follows a client’s calendar, and the worker handles their own taxes. Delivery and rideshare are local. In the Bankrate side-hustle survey they were a small share of the named types.

FlexJobs, entry-level remote jobs, updated 28 May 2026. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook, May 2025, and OEWS table 1 for tutors. Named ads were reviewed in September and October 2026 as examples of arrangements. They are not openings from Find Curate.
